Who We Are

We Turn Ideas Into Impressions.

At Dadris Graphix Printing & Signs, creativity meets craftsmanship. We help businesses, organisations and individuals bring their ideas to life through thoughtful design, quality printing, branding and signage.

Our Story

Creative Thinking. Practical Solutions. Lasting Impressions.

Dadris Graphix Printing & Signs was built around a simple idea: helping people and businesses communicate better through creative design and professional visual solutions.

We believe every brand has a story worth telling. Whether a small business is looking for its first identity, an organisation is preparing promotional materials, or a company needs eye-catching outdoor signage, every project deserves attention and creativity.

Based in Ogalo, Kakamega County, we bring together creative design, printing and fabrication to deliver solutions that help clients present themselves with confidence. We serve customers locally and across Kenya.

We don't just make designs. We help turn ideas into something people can see, recognise and remember.
